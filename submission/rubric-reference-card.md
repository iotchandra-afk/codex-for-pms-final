# ROOK DISPATCH 4.2

## A healthier average did not mean a recovered service.

**Decision:** validate a bounded readiness-and-recovery pilot, measure appropriate offer exposure alongside acceptance, and preserve incident-assignment and Supply safeguards.

### 01 · Application of Concepts

**One business problem, six connected methods.** Company context framed the questions; four interviews and 147 tickets exposed different experiences; 1,696 pings tested the pattern; routing-code inspection reproduced a mechanism; a working prototype made recovery tangible; a reusable skill established a review habit.

**Rubric takeaway:** each module changed the next decision.

[Evidence chain and six exact prompt/result pairs](../final-report.html#journey)

### 02 · Credibility & Reasoning

**The conclusion is traceable—and its limits are explicit.** Acceptance moved from 76.8% at baseline to 54.2% in the mixed release week and 72.7% in the final week. Four responders' combined offers fell from a baseline mean of 49/week to three in the final week. All 160 responder-weeks, including zero-offer weeks, were retained.

A controlled 75-second response succeeds at 90 seconds and misses at 60. Actual historical eligibility, scores and full causal attribution remain unknown.

**Rubric takeaway:** distinguish observed impact, reproduced behavior and unverified explanation.

### 03 · Clarity

**Make the decision easy to inspect.** The final presentation pairs six real results with the exact user prompts behind them, explains the failure visually, and embeds the actual clickable prototype. A one-page brief states the problem, proposed experience, scope and rollout gates. Counts, quotations and raw rows remain accessible.

**Rubric takeaway:** a reviewer can move from recommendation to evidence to experience.

### 04 · Strategic Thinking

**Restore appropriate opportunity while protecting service quality.** Ready Review combines responder confirmation, incident-fit and maintenance checks, a bounded opportunity and an auditable receipt. Allocation is never guaranteed; proposed review windows and pilot thresholds require approval.

The weekly skill checks **Owner · Outcome · Scope · Problem first**. Two actual runs found four unresolved brief problems. It strengthens commitment decisions; responder-level monitoring and engineering release validation provide separate safeguards.

**Rubric takeaway:** connect customer trust, operational tradeoffs, accountability and measurable recovery.
